Signals flow upward: inputs sit on a part’s bottom edge, outputs on its top edge. Wires work both ways and join pins into one shared net. Drag from any pin to another pin to wire. Drag empty space to pan the endless canvas; the mouse wheel or the zoom bar zooms. Drag a part by its body to move it. Controls (lever switch, button circle, sensor dot, delay or sustain setting, selector Next, clock squares) only react to a tap on the control itself, never to a drag. Go to 0,0 recentres the view on the canvas origin (marked with a small cross). Select turns on multi-select: drag empty space to box-select, tap parts to add or remove them, and drag a selected part to move the whole group. Then use Copy, Paste (lands at the centre of the view), Duplicate or Delete. Wires between selected parts come along. Turning Select off clears the selection but keeps the copied parts for pasting. Rotate (or the R key) turns the selected part, or every part in a selection, a quarter turn clockwise, and the pins turn with it.
