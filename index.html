Help
100%
  • Add a part. Drag its name from the bar onto the canvas and let go where you want it. Or just tap its name to drop it in the middle of the view.
  • Move a part. Drag its body. It snaps to a grid.
  • Edit a wire. Tap a wire. Dots appear on it: drag a hollow dot to pull out a bend, drag a filled dot to move it, tap a filled dot to remove it. A slim bar at the bottom changes its color: Dark Grey (turns light blue when powered), five Colored wires and nine Neon wires. Path: Manual (in the toolbar) lets you place bends while drawing a new wire: drag from a pin, let go in empty space to drop a bend, tap more spots, then tap a pin to finish.
  • Connect. Drag from a pin to another pin. To remove a wire, tap it, then tap Delete.
  • Signals go up. Inputs are on the bottom edge, outputs on the top. On pins turn green. On wires get brighter with white dots.
  • Wires work both ways. All pins joined by wires act as one connection. If any output in it is on, every pin in it is on. Wire colors are only for looks.
  • Look around. Drag empty space to pan. Use the wheel or the zoom bar to zoom. Go to 0,0 (under More) returns to the cross at the center.
  • Controls. Lever, button circle, sensor dot, delay or sustain box, Next and clock squares react only to a tap on the control. Drag the part by another spot.
  • Run, Pause, Step. The slider sets steps per second. Reset state restores the starting state.
  • Select. Tap Select, then drag a box around parts or tap parts to toggle them. Drag one to move the group. Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Delete and Rotate work on all selected parts, and wires between them come along. Paste lands mid-view.
  • Rotate. Select a part and tap Rotate (or press R) for a clockwise quarter turn.
  • Saving. Work autosaves in this browser. Saves keeps named versions and starts new projects.
  • Lasers. A beam leaves the top edge and stops at the first part in its way. Wires do not stop it. A Laser Detector is on while the beam hits its bottom (sensor) edge.
  • Displays. Place a Converter directly under a Display of the same size (7-Seg with 7-Seg, 14-Seg with 14-Seg) so the pins touch. No wires needed. Tap Key for a guide, or hold over a pin to see its name.
  • Selectors. Outputs count from right to left, so output 1 is on the right.
  • Learn a part. Tutorial explains any part. Logic basics introduces on and off signals and gates.
